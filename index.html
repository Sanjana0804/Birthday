A LETTER FOR MY CHILDHOOD BEST FRIEND

Dear Bestie, 🤍

Sometimes I sit and think about how it has been more than 10 years since we became friends, and honestly... I don't know where all that time went.

It feels like yesterday when we were just two kids, worrying about the smallest things and fighting over the most ridiculous topics. 😂 And how can I forget our first proper fight in 8th class? Jab bhi mujhe yaad aati hai, I can't stop laughing. Seriously, hum kaisi kaisi baaton par ladte the! At that time those things felt so serious, aur ab sochti hoon toh bas hasi aati hai. 😂

But somewhere between all those silly fights, random conversations, school days, laughter, photos, and countless memories... we grew up.

Aur pata hi nahi chala kab. 🤍

Pata hi nahi chala kab woh bachon wali choti-choti baaton par ladne wale hum, itni samajhdari wali baatein karne lage.

Kab hum sirf school gossip discuss karne se life goals discuss karne lage.

Kab "kal kya homework hai?" se "future mein kya karna hai" tak baat pahunch gayi.

Kab humne apne family issues, problems, fears aur life ki tensions ek doosre ke saath share karni shuru kar di.

Aur sabse strange baat? Pata hi nahi chala kab hum itne bade ho gaye. 🌷

Pehle school se bahar kuch lene jana bhi ek adventure hota tha. Ab hum khud bahar jaate hain, ghoomte hain, apne plans banate hain, aur life ke decisions ke baare mein baatein karte hain.

We spent so much time waiting to grow up... and now when I look back at our old pictures, I realise that we actually did.

Those two kids who used to fight over silly things are still somewhere inside us. Bas ab fights ke topics thode upgrade ho gaye hain. 😂

But honestly, one of the most beautiful things about growing up has been knowing that you were there through so many parts of it.

You are not just someone I have known for more than 10 years. You are a part of my childhood, my school life, my embarrassing phases 😂, my laughter, my silly memories, and so many moments that I will always carry with me.

When I look at our old pictures, I don't just see photos.

I see two kids who had absolutely no idea about how much life was going to change.

They didn't know that one day they would sit and discuss life, future, family, problems, goals, and everything that once felt so far away.

Maybe that's what makes these memories so special.

We didn't realise we were making memories while we were busy living them. 🤍

Thank you for being there through more than ten years of my life. Through the silly fights, the random laughter, the embarrassing photos, the serious conversations, and all the versions of ourselves that existed in between.

Life will keep changing, we will keep growing, and who knows where life will take us next...

But I hope that no matter how much time passes, whenever we look back at these memories, we will always remember those two kids who had no idea that their friendship would last this long. 🌷

And honestly...

I am really grateful that I got to grow up with you. 🤍

Happy Birthday to my childhood best friend, my partner in countless memories, and the person with whom I somehow survived more than 10 years of friendship despite all our stupid fights. 😂🎂🎀

Here's to our childhood, our memories, our growth, and to all the new adventures waiting for us.

We grew up... but please promise we never grow up too much. 😂🤍🌸
